Step 2 · Prove · What we do

Ten weeks. One line. One clear question.

The blind spot the Analyze found, tested on the floor with real data, and judged against criteria agreed before anyone starts.

The question

Written as hypotheses, not as hopes

The Analyze names three or more hypotheses about what drives quality and throughput on the line. Each is phrased so it can be confirmed or rejected with data, and none is added later.

How it runs

Set up, capture, correlate, decide

Log points and QR codes placed and field-tested (wk 1–2); operator rounds every shift (wk 3–7); outcomes tagged back to conditions on the timeline (wk 6–9); results reviewed together (wk 10).

How it is judged

On the criteria, in one session

At week ten we sit down with the numbers against the criteria. A hypothesis that is rejected is still a result: it removes a theory the plant has been carrying for years.
