The opportunity

Cause and effect never end up on the same timeline.

Your sensors capture the machine. What they do not capture is what the people around it see, and that is where the unexplained variation sits.

These operational blind spots follow the same pattern in every plant:

01

Something changes on the line

A different infeed, a machine running slightly off, an operator stepping in to correct it.

02

The effect shows up later

In scrap, a stop or a lab result. Hours or days after whatever caused it.

03

You retrace it from what was recorded

Which is the machine data, and never the reason behind it. So improvement stays reactive.

Close that gap and the blind spot becomes the most improvable part of the plant.
