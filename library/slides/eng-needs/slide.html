What we need from you

We build the project team together. It grows with the step.

Typically these people, not necessarily these titles. What matters is that the process knowledge and the floor are both represented.

Step 1 · Analyze · 2–3 weeks

A small core, mostly off the floor

  • Typically a project lead, a process engineer and an operations lead
  • Access to the data the plant already keeps, under an NDA we sign first
  • Time a few hours in total, plus one review session at the end
Step 2 · Prove · ten weeks

The same core, plus the floor

  • Adds a sponsor and two or three operators from the shifts on the line
  • Site access for set-up, operator training and field testing
  • Time minutes per shift for operators, about an hour a week for the sponsor, a feedback round every two weeks

Everything else is on us: configuration, training, device support, analysis and reporting. An annual agreement asks for a different team again, and that is scoped when we get there.
