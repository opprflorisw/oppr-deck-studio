Step 1 · Analyze · What we do

We start with the data you already have.

Before anything is installed or captured, we read what the plant has already written down, and find out whether there is a case worth proving.

What we look at

The data you already keep

ERP, historians, LIMS, downtime registers, Excel files and paper logs. We inventory and ingest it; nothing new is collected yet, and no hardware goes near the line.

How we work

Mostly remote, one visit on site

Two to three weeks from the moment we have access, under an NDA signed first. A short quick scan on site to see the process run, then analysis off site with your process engineer on call.

Where it lands

A scoped Proof of Value, or an honest no

If the value case is there, we hand over the scope for it. If it is not, we say so, and you spent € 10.000 finding that out instead of € 25.000.
