Annex B

The annual-subscription documents.

If the Proof converts to an annual agreement, these come into play.

Core agreements

  • Order Form · the annual platform licence
  • Services SOW · statement of work for implementation and guidance

Framework & change

  • MSSA · master services and subscription agreement, the general terms
  • Change Order · scope changes or an extension

Data & service

  • DPA · data processing agreement, EU data processing
  • SLA · service level agreement, uptime and support
  • AUP · acceptable use, including AI

Security & confidentiality

  • Security Policy / TOMs · technical and organisational measures
  • NDA · mutual confidentiality

Where you bring your own procurement, security or site documents, we review them and align the details together.
