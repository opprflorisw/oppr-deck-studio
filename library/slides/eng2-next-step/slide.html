The Analyze comes in two parts, and ends in a scope.

1

Working session

The opening of the Analyze, not a separate meeting. We agree the areas of interest and what data exists, so the analysis has a target.

2

The data analysis

We read what the plant already keeps. The report comes back with the findings and with scope options for the Proof of Value.

3

You choose the scope

Together we pick one of the options and sign it off. The Proof of Value then runs on exactly that, and nothing else.

Scoping is what makes the Proof of Value worth running, and the scope is an output of the Analyze, never an assumption made before it.
