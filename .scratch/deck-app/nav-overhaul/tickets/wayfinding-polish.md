# Wayfinding polish

**Type:** grilling · **Blocked by:** app-role, unified-archive · **Status:** open

## Question

The cross-cutting "does it feel smooth" pass, once the structural decisions land:

- Back / breadcrumb behaviour on detail pages (slide detail, graphic detail) and
  from the intake flow.
- Viewer consistency: the paged viewer vs the post-text editor vs inline design-
  system stacking — one predictable "look at a thing" pattern.
- Empty states and first-run (nothing in a filter, no drafts, no archived).
- Remembered state (tabs, filters, view modes) — audit what persists and whether
  that is what a returning user expects.

Kept last because most of it depends on the final IA and the archive home.
