## Question

What does the LinkedIn *post* for a welcome say, as a repeatable template?

The cartoon is the image; this is the text that carries it. Welcome posts are a
genre with a shape. Define the template so it is generated in the Oppr voice, not
freestyled each time:

- The variables: name, role, one or two lines about the person or what they will
  work on.
- The Oppr voice constraints already in `knowledge/best-practices/linkedin-post.md`
  (hook before the fold, Unicode bold sparingly and never on names - relevant here,
  0-3 hashtags, single blank lines, personal-profile posting).
- Tone: warm and human, but still Oppr (short, declarative, no hype).
- Whether it ends with an ask (tag them, say hi) or is purely celebratory.
- How much is templated boilerplate vs generated per person.

Independent of the image recipe. Unblocked.
