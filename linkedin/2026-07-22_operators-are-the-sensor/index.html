01 · Capture

The signal that matters most is walking the floor.

Sensors log temperature, speed, output. They miss the context: the workaround, the near miss, the “it always does that on the night shift”. Your operators are the sensor for everything the machine can’t register.

02 · Connect

Machine data says what. Operators say why.

Put both on one timeline and the pattern shows up: the stop, and the reason behind it. That is where an improvement stops being a guess and becomes something you can prove.
